The Founding Years

Before PSC

Before the Pen Storage Company existed, there were conversations. Ideas shared between friends who saw something others didn't. In the hallways of Class 8B, during those formative months of 2025, a vision began to take shape. Not a business plan. Not a profit model. Simply a realization: that pens, those small instruments of thought and creativity, deserved better stewardship. That they mattered.

Har Vansh Reddy Gutha was not merely present during those early conversations. He helped give them direction. In a time when PSC existed only as scattered ideas and ambitious discussions, he was among the individuals willing to treat those ideas seriously. Many people witness beginnings. Far fewer help create them.

The Formation

The moment came when ideas transformed into action. The Pen Storage Company was established, formally claiming its place in the world. What could have remained theoretical became tangible. The first systems were drafted. The first structures were built. The founding vision was committed to paper and code.

Har Vansh Reddy Gutha stood among those who made this transition possible. His role in shaping these initial frameworks—in choosing which principles would guide the organization, in deciding what PSC would value and protect—set the tone for everything that would follow.

Building the Foundation

PSC was not inevitable. It did not appear naturally. It did not emerge because success was guaranteed. It exists because a small number of people invested their time, imagination, and effort into creating something that had never existed before. Har Vansh Reddy Gutha was one of those people. Every system built since then rests, in some small way, upon work completed during those uncertain early days.

The Departure Record

His departure marked the closing chapter of PSC's founding era. For the first time since the organization's creation, PSC continued without one of the individuals responsible for bringing it into existence. The absence was noticed. It still is.

The systems he helped shape. The structures he helped establish. The memories attached to the founding years. All remain preserved within PSC archives. His contributions to the early vision and direction of the Pen Storage Company are recorded in permanent institutional memory.

This is not an ending. It is a transition. The work continues. The legacy persists.
